Core entities

Every treatment starts life as a consultation — the consulting doctor examines the patient, decides what's needed, and assigns whoever performs it. A patient can run several treatments over their lifetime at the clinic, each one traceable back to the consultation that spawned it, and each one able to change hands to a different doctor mid-way (logged as a handoff). Billing happens at two levels: a flat fee per consultation, and a per-visit charge that gets settled as it happens — the treatment's final invoice only rolls up whatever's still unpaid when it's marked finished. The prescription is no longer write-once — the doctor who wrote an entry can revise it later, with every prior version kept.

Who can do what

Same actions for both roles — the difference is scope. Admin sees the whole clinic; a Doctor sees their own assigned cases. Both can add a prescription entry at any visit.

Open questions

Things worth pinning down before this turns into code.

  • Who applies the discount The doctor negotiates in the room — do they enter the discounted price directly on the visit, or does it route through Admin for approval past a certain amount?
  • Is a prior consultation ever skippable Confirmed: every new treatment starts with a fresh consultation, even for a returning patient. Worth double-checking there's truly no exception — e.g. a same-day emergency walk-in with no time for a formal exam first.
  • Consultation fee — fixed constant or a catalog entry It's ₹500 flat today. Does it live in the SERVICE catalog like everything else (so Admin can change it later without a code change), or is it a separate configurable setting?
  • Handoff reason — required or optional When a treatment's doctor changes mid-way, is a reason mandatory for the record, or can it be left blank and just show who/when?
  • No GST fields on INVOICE Indian clinics generally need CGST/SGST split, GSTIN, and HSN/SAC codes on invoices, with therapeutic procedures often GST-exempt and cosmetic ones taxed. Worth deciding whether that's needed for v1 or added later.
  • Consent forms for procedures Procedures like RCT/extraction typically need a recorded, signed consent — separate from the clinical prescription notes. Not modeled yet.
  • Allergies & current medications Right now these would sit inside the free-text medical_history field. Worth pulling into their own structured fields on PATIENT so they can be checked before prescribing, rather than buried in prose.

Deferred to a later phase

Confirmed as needed eventually — not blocking the current build, since Admin/Doctor panels come first and the patient-facing side comes after.

  • Patient-facing login & appointment booking No patient login in this phase. When the patient system gets integrated later, it brings appointment booking with it — a calendar, future slots, and (per Indian-clinic norms) WhatsApp/SMS reminders.
  • Doctor availability / scheduling Seeing what each doctor is assigned to at what time, and when they're next free — this rides on the same appointment system above, so it waits until that's built.
  • Capacitor-wrapped mobile app Already accounted for in the system shape (same React/Vite build, wrapped by Capacitor) — just flagging that it's a real deliverable for later, not dropped.
